Privacy Policy — Widget Loom

Package: com.pierre89vdb.widgetloom · Developer: Pierre Vandenberghe · Effective date: November 20, 2026

Widget Loom is designed with strict respect for your privacy. The app contains no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, no crash trackers, and no user accounts. We do not collect, sell, rent, or store any of your personal data on our own servers.

1. Permissions & How Data Is Used

  • Calendar (READ_CALENDAR — optional): Used solely when you enable the Calendar Schedule widget to read upcoming events from the calendars you select on your device. Calendar events are processed strictly on-device for widget rendering and are never transmitted over the network.
  • Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION — optional): Used only if you ask the Golden Hour or Hourly Weather widget to fetch your current location (while in the app or when you manually refresh/configure GPS). Widget Loom does not request or use background location. You can instead pick any city or enter coordinates manually. When Weather updates, only your selected latitude and longitude are sent to the configured weather provider to retrieve forecasts.
  • Alarms (SET_ALARM & Clock queries — optional): Used solely to read your next scheduled system alarm time for display inside the Calendar Schedule widget (when enabled) and to open your device's Clock app on tap.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Used exclusively by the Hourly Weather widget to fetch forecasts and optional city search results from the selected weather data provider.

2. Third-Party Weather Data Providers

Depending on your widget configuration, geographic coordinates (or city query text) are sent directly from your device to:

  • MET Norway (Yr.no Locationforecast): Default weather provider. Subject to MET Norway's terms and privacy policy (api.met.no). Weather data is provided under CC BY 4.0.
  • OpenWeatherMap (optional, bring-your-own API key): Only contacted if you explicitly select OpenWeatherMap and enter your own API key. Your personal API key is stored locally on your device and excluded from cloud backups.

3. Local Storage & Data Retention

Widget preferences, selected calendars, custom coordinates, optional OpenWeatherMap API keys, and temporary weather response caches are stored solely on your device in Android DataStore and SharedPreferences, and excluded from Android Auto Backup rules. Uninstalling Widget Loom permanently removes all stored data from your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Widget Loom does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction).

5. Changes to This Policy

If future features alter how permissions or data are handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes are released.
